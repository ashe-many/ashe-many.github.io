Drop-Aware Fine-Tuning Pipeline

Is the 4-point loss at 75% token removal caused by lost image data, or by input the model never saw in training? Two LoRA runs answer this. Started 14 Nov, 20:55; finished 15 Nov, 06:24. Answer: mostly lost image data.

MoE Token Compressor

Done 16 Nov, 13:57. Result: MoE 86.0% with 19.3 tokens (target: more than 90% with fewer than 50). Expert 48 alone: 89.3% with 48 tokens. Three trained compressors ("experts") make 16, 48 or 128 image tokens from the ≈ 269 tokens of the merger. A router reads the question and picks one expert. Target: more than 90% POPE accuracy with a mean of fewer than 50 tokens. The base model does not change; all new parts are added.

Question-Aware MoE

Done 17 Nov, 00:35. Result: no gain. No checkpoint was better than the old MoE on held-out images, so the fallback kept the old MoE. The POPE test gave the same 86.0% again. The experts also read the question. Thus the 16 or 48 tokens collect the image parts that the question asks about. The router and the experts train together. Target: more than 90% POPE accuracy with fewer than 20 tokens. At the start, the new model is the same as the old MoE. If the new model is not better on held-out images, the old experts stay.

Beyond POPE

Done on 17 Nov, 19:21. Result: on GQA, expert 48 is close to all tokens. On TextVQA, all compression fails. Does expert 48 also work on real tasks? GQA asks about objects and their relations. TextVQA asks the model to read text in the image. This is the hardest case for compression. No new training: the experts are the same as in the MoE tab.

How to read · drag to move
1. The boxes
  • The boxes show the steps from left to right: data, training, outputs, evaluation.
  • A green check mark tells you that the step is finished. Click a box to see its result below the diagram.
  • An orange box is a step where image tokens are removed.
2. The three runs
  • A: the original model. No training.
  • B: trained with all image tokens. This is the control.
  • C: trained with 0–75% of the image tokens removed at random.
  • All three runs answer the same 300 POPE questions, with 0, 25, 50, 75 and 100% of the tokens removed.
3. The question

Run A loses 4 points at 75% removed. There are two possible causes:

  • New input form: the model never saw input with tokens removed. If this is the cause, C fixes the loss.
  • Lost image data: the removed tokens hold data that the model needs. If this is the cause, training cannot fix the loss.
4. The answer
  • C still loses 3 points (90.0% → 87.0%). A loses 4 (91.3% → 87.3%). C does not fix the loss.
  • Result: the loss comes from lost image data.
5. Other results
  • B is worse at 75% removed (83.3%). Normal training makes the model less tolerant of removed tokens.
  • C stays tolerant (87.0%). If you train, train with tokens removed.
  • Up to 50% removed, all runs stay near 90%. Half of the image tokens can go with almost no loss.
  • At 100% removed, all runs say "no" to all questions (≈ 51%, the share of No answers).
6. The p-values (box "Compare")
  • p below 0.05: a real difference. p above 0.05: the difference can be chance.
  • Real differences: B worse than A at 75% (p = 0.002); C better than B at 50% (p = 0.03) and 75% (p = 0.001).
7. Limits
  • 300 questions, 1,000 training steps, one seed. A gain of less than about 3 points cannot be seen.
